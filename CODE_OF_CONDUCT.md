# Code of conduct

Haze follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies to the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions and any other place where people
work on Haze together.

In short: be respectful and patient with other people, assume good faith, and keep criticism
about the work rather than the person. Harassment, insults and personal attacks aren't
acceptable, and neither is publishing someone's private information.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior makes you uncomfortable, email ritabratamaiti@gmail.com. Reports are read
by the maintainer and kept confidential. You'll get a reply, and if action is needed, it follows
the enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant: a warning, a temporary ban, or a
permanent ban, depending on how serious the behavior is and whether it continues.
